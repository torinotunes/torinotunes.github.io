Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

"Torino Uploader" is a private tool used only by the operator of the Torino Tunes YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uploads video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, playlist entries, and publishing schedules to the owner's own YouTube channel, and reads back the status of those videos.

How data is stored

The access token is stored only on the operator's own computer. The tool does not collect, sell, or share any personal data, and it does not access any other user's data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Please contact us through the Torino Tunes YouTube channel.
